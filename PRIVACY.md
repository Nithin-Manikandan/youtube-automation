# Privacy policy

"youtube automation" is a personal tool used by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- It uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos and thumbnails to the channel of the Google account that authorised it.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people. There are no other users.
- The only credential kept is the owner's OAuth refresh token, stored as a private secret in the owner's own GitHub repository.
- Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
